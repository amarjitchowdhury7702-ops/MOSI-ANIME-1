📖 Who is Korin?

Korin (called Karin in Japan) is a wise, white cat who has lived for more than 800 years at the top of the very tall Korin Tower. Fighters around the world know him as the "God of Martial Arts", so people are often surprised to find that the legend is a small cat with a walking stick.

According to the guidebook Daizenshuu 7, Korin came from the Other World and was sent to Earth to help Kami, Earth's guardian. He acts like he doesn't care about the world below his tower, but he always helps when things get serious. He likes playing tricks and testing people, and he's a bit lazy, but underneath he's a kind teacher.

Senzu BeansHe is the only one who grows them. One bean heals any injury and fills you up for about 10 days.
Flying NimbusHe controls the source cloud of Goku's Flying Nimbus, and once gave Mercenary Tao a trick Dark Nimbus.

🗼 The Sacred Water Legend

  • Master Roshi

    As a young man, Roshi was the first to climb the tower. It took him 3 years to grab the Sacred Water from Korin.

  • Goku arrives

    Goku climbs the tower to get stronger after Mercenary Tao kills his friend. Korin keeps dodging him and dares him to take the water.

  • A test of honour

    Goku could have stolen the water while Korin "slept", but he chose to win fairly. Korin was only pretending to sleep, and he was so impressed that he decided to train Goku properly.

  • The secret

    After 3 days, Goku catches the bottle. It turns out the "Sacred Water" is just ordinary tap water. What really made Goku stronger was the training: climbing the tower and chasing Korin.

✨ Fun Facts

  • Akira Toriyama based Korin on his own pet cat and drew him while the cat was asleep. That's why Korin's eyes are always closed.
  • Korin has blue fur in the original manga but white fur in the anime and the full-colour manga.
  • A later cat of Toriyama's inspired Beerus, the God of Destruction in Dragon Ball Super.
  • According to Dragon Ball: Adventure Special, Korin can't stand dogs.
  • His tower sits right under The Lookout, so climbing it is the way up to meet Kami.
